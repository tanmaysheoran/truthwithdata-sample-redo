How to Use

  1. Enter your numerical data values separated by commas, spaces, or new lines
  2. Click "Calculate Statistics" to see the results
  3. Review the mean, median, mode, and other descriptive statistics

Understanding Descriptive Statistics

  • Mean: The average of all values (sum divided by count)
  • Median: The middle value when data is sorted (50th percentile)
  • Mode: The most frequently occurring value(s)
  • Range: The difference between maximum and minimum values
  • Count: The total number of data points
